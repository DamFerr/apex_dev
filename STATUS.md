Joins (`related`): same label, or a bare kind with a mix of it (On-Hit + On-Hit / AD); different mixes never join. Cause of "One build so far" (real log): 6450 of 7040 Kai'Sa games were labelled On-Hit / AP because Guinsoo's counted as AP.
